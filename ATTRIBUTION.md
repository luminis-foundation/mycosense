# Attribution Requirements — MycoSense

## When Attribution Is Required

Visible attribution is required for all public-facing uses of MycoSense,
including but not limited to:

- Web dashboards and applications
- Public deployments and demonstrations
- Academic papers, posters, and presentations
- Grant applications and funding materials
- Hosted services and cloud platforms
- Derivative products and forks
- Documentation and technical reports
- Datasets published using MycoSense pipelines

## Required Attribution

Include the following attribution in a visible, reasonable location:

> **Based on MycoSense, originally developed by Luminis Foundation.**

For academic citations, use:

> Garcia, C. (2026). *Bridging Mycelium-Inspired Decentralized Computing and
> Symbiotic Plant–Fungal–AI Bio-Hybrid Systems in Northern New Mexico's
> High-Desert Ecosystems.* Zenodo.
> [https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20143391](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20143391)

## Placement

- **Web applications:** Footer, about page, or credits section
- **Papers and presentations:** Acknowledgments or references section
- **Devices and hardware:** Product documentation or packaging
- **Hosted services:** Terms of service, about page, or documentation
- **Datasets:** README or metadata accompanying the dataset

## What Attribution Does Not Grant

Attribution does not imply endorsement, certification, partnership, or official
status with Luminis Foundation. See `TRADEMARK.md` for trademark
restrictions.
