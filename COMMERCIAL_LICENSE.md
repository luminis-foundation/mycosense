# Commercial License — MycoSense

## Commercial Use Requires a Separate Written License

Any use of MycoSense that does not qualify as noncommercial research, education,
nonprofit, personal, or public-benefit use requires a separate written commercial
license agreement from Luminis Foundation Research.

## Categories Requiring a Commercial License

- **Software Products** — incorporating MycoSense into revenue-generating
  applications
- **Hosted Services / SaaS** — offering MycoSense functionality as a service
- **Device Manufacturing** — embedding MycoSense firmware or software in
  commercially sold hardware, sensors, or monitoring devices
- **Commercial Monitoring & Analytics** — using MycoSense for paid environmental
  monitoring, data analytics, or consulting services
- **Dataset Licensing** — creating and selling datasets derived from MycoSense
  data pipelines
- **Consulting & Professional Services** — using MycoSense as a core component
  of paid consulting or contracting work
- **Derivative Products** — building commercial products derived from the
  MycoSense codebase, architecture, or firmware

## Commercial License Terms

Commercial license agreements may include the following terms, negotiated on a
case-by-case basis:

| Term | Description |
|---|---|
| **Visible Attribution** | Required in all commercial deployments |
| **Annual License Fees** | Based on scope, scale, and use case |
| **Revenue Share** | Percentage of revenue from MycoSense-derived products |
| **Device / Manufacturing Royalties** | Per-unit royalties for hardware products |
| **Hosted-Service / SaaS Terms** | Usage-based or subscription pricing |
| **Dataset Licensing Terms** | Fees for commercial dataset distribution |
| **Nonprofit / Public-Benefit Discounts** | Reduced terms for mission-aligned organizations |
| **No Implied Endorsement** | Commercial licensees may not imply Foundation endorsement |
| **Written Approval Required** | All commercial licenses require written approval by authorized Foundation officers |

## How to Apply

To request a commercial license, contact Luminis Foundation Research:

1. Describe your intended commercial use
2. Specify the scope (number of deployments, users, devices, etc.)
3. Identify your organization and its relationship to the use case

License inquiries:
- **Organization:** Luminis Foundation Research
- **Repository:** [github.com/luminis-foundation/mycosense](https://github.com/luminis-foundation/mycosense)
